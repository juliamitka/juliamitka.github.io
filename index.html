About me

As a Product Designer, I work on complex B2B products where good design means finding the right balance between visual quality, clear structure, and solid logic. I enjoy taking complicated processes and turning them into something simpler and more intuitive.

I've always liked bringing order to chaos. I enjoy digging into problems, organising information, exploring different options, and finding solutions that make sense both for users and the business. My background in system analysis probably has something to do with that.

I studied Finance and Accounting at the Krakow University of Economics and started my career in IT as a System Analyst. I later moved into UX/UI at Kaczmarski Group, where I helped build the UX/UI role within the organisation.

Outside of work, I'm usually somewhere between planning my next trip, meeting friends, and enjoying a quiet evening at home. I love cooking, baking sourdough bread, and making things with my own hands. Lately, I've been exploring a few creative projects of my own, including designing and making personalized tote bags. I've also started creating wedding websites, with the goal of eventually turning this passion into a small business.
